## Change requested and implemented

Restores the original M2 funnel profile rather than the previous Ellis-only presentation.
Its surround extends into a broad, nearly flat Cartesian grid, clipped to a square of half
extent 24. Default upper region; optional connected lower region. The accepted static M2
study remains unchanged. Thirteen simultaneous balls start at x=-18 with z=-4.8…4.8,
heading +X. Each initial tangent is normalized to unit intrinsic surface speed. Playback
starts at 4×. Row placement, individual starting positions, common heading, throat radius,
funnel depth, selected-ball follow, visibility and existing transport controls are available.

Removed the display-boundary pause. The finite mesh is a viewing region, not a physical
barrier. Balls continue outside it, with bounded trail history; follow selected ball remains
available. Balls passing through are not deleted or described as gravitationally captured.

## Mathematical model

The previous pure Ellis reference remains available and tested. The restored funnel has
a different induced metric; its equations must change with its actual shape.

Let a be throat radius, d funnel depth, reference radius R₀=10, and
b=d/acosh(R₀/a). Use signed l and periodic φ:

    r = sqrt(l²+a²)
    X = (r cos φ, b asinh(l/a) − d, r sin φ)

At r=R₀ the upper patch has height zero, and at the throat height −d. This exactly matches
the old M2 funnel on its original radial domain. Extension is smooth and asymptotically
nearly flat; it is not forcibly flattened. Both regions join smoothly at l=0.

Differentiating X gives E=g_ll=(l²+b²)/(l²+a²), G=g_φφ=l²+a² and zero cross term.
From the Euler–Lagrange equations for (E l′²+G φ′²)/2:

    l″ = l(b²−a²)/[(l²+a²)(l²+b²)] l′²
         + l(l²+a²)/(l²+b²) φ′²
    φ″ = −2l/(l²+a²) l′φ′

Conserved quantities in the continuous equations are energy E l′²+G φ′² and
J=Gφ′. Unit-speed throat crossing has critical |J|=a. When b=a the original Ellis
metric/equations are recovered. This is a spatial surface model, not photon motion,
gravitational lensing or a spacetime simulation.

CPU Double RK4 uses the existing fixed 1/240 step and bounded catch-up. Initial planar
heading is lifted into the surface tangent plane and normalized by the metric. Angular
wrapping does not interrupt world-space trails. Restart is deterministic under the documented
compiler/precision assumptions; numerical conservation is approximate.

## Architecture and bounded resources

Core: EllisWormhole.swift (optional original-funnel profile), WormholeComparison.swift
(beam initialization and independent fixed-step tracks). UI: WormholeSession.swift,
WormholeInspector.swift and existing shared SurfaceViewport/SurfaceMotionSession.
Rendering: existing cached surface mesh, Cartesian grid shader and batched GeodesicTrails.
Each track has a separate buffer lane; shared pipeline/encoder and three in-flight slots.
Torus defaults and single-track rendering API are retained.

Maximum 17 tracks × 8192 samples × 16 bytes × 3 slots = 6,684,672 bytes of trail upload
storage, calculated from source, not a measured footprint. Default 13 tracks use at most
26 overlay draws. No benchmark or sustained thermal performance is claimed.
